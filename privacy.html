Privacy Policy

Insider's Eyes · Last updated 23 August 2026

The short version

Insider's Eyes does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics. We do not know who you are.

What the app does

The app downloads a public file of insider stock transactions and displays it. Those transactions come from Form 4 filings that corporate insiders are legally required to submit to the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, and which the SEC publishes at sec.gov. The app reformats public records. Nothing you do in the app is sent anywhere.

What we collect

Nothing. Specifically: no name, email, phone number, contacts, location, photos, files, device identifiers, or advertising identifiers. We have no servers that receive data from you and no database of users.

Watchlists and settings

Any preferences you set are stored only on your device. They are not uploaded and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Network requests

The app fetches a JSON file from feed.insiderseyes.app. Like any web request, our hosting provider may log the IP address and time of the request as part of ordinary server operation. Those logs are not linked to any identity and are not used to build a profile of you.

Links to the SEC

The app links to filings on sec.gov. Once you follow such a link you are on a U.S. government website governed by its own privacy practices, not ours.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version of the app collects anything — for example if advertising or push notifications are added — this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described here rather than made quietly.

Not financial advice

Insider's Eyes displays public regulatory filings. It does not provide investment, financial, or trading advice, and does not recommend any security. Insiders may lawfully trade their own company's stock provided they report it; illegal insider trading is trading on material non-public information, which is a different thing entirely and not what this app shows.
